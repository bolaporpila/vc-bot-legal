Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VC Bot handles information when you use the service.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Information We Collect

VC Bot may receive technical information provided through Discord that is necessary for the bot to operate correctly, including information related to servers, channels and the bot's connection.

How We Use Information

Information may be used when necessary to provide, maintain and improve the functionality of VC Bot. We do not sell personal information.

Voice Data

VC Bot does not intentionally record, store or distribute conversations or audio from Discord voice channels.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed when necessary to operate the service or when required by law.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information necessary for the operation of VC Bot.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Important changes may be reflected in a new version of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the Discord server where VC Bot is installed.
